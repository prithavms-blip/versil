The problem

When bleeding can't be pressed, today's tools fall short.

Uncontrolled hemorrhage is the leading cause of preventable death after injury. For deep, noncompressible wounds — torso, groin, neck — gauze has to be packed by hand, washes out when wet, and depends on the patient still being able to clot. First responders need something faster, simpler, and more reliable.

The technology

One material that travels like a liquid and holds like a solid.

Dispensed straight into the wound, Versil's foam expands into a sponge that fills the cavity, applies outward pressure, and actively drives clotting — all at once.

Seals & clots at once

Applies pressure and triggers clotting together — working even when the patient's own blood can't clot.

One-handed, no training

No packing, no technique to learn. Reaches deep wounds that can't be packed and sets in under 30 seconds.

Works in the field

Waterproof and field-stable where gauze fails — rain, snow, or wet wounds — and removes cleanly in one piece.
